What We Offer

Everything You Need

Two powerful tools to help you understand your health and take informed action.

🩺
Symptom Checker
Select your symptoms from 58 options across 5 categories. Our ML model instantly predicts the most likely disease with confidence score and severity level.
🗂️
Disease Database
Browse all 28 diseases our system can detect. Click any disease card to see full details, symptoms, severity info, precautions, and when to see a doctor.
📋
Prediction History
Every prediction you make is saved in your personal history panel in the Symptom Checker. Track patterns and monitor changes over time.
How It Works

6 Simple Steps

01
🔐
Register & Login
Create your account and log in securely to your personal dashboard.
02
📋
Select Symptoms
Choose from 58 categorised symptoms that match what you are feeling.
03
⚙️
Data Conversion
Symptoms are converted into binary numerical vectors for the ML model.
04
🤖
Model Processes
Random Forest / Naive Bayes / SVM analyses your symptom pattern.
05
🎯
Disease Predicted
The most probable disease is identified with a confidence percentage.
06
💊
Get Guidance
Receive severity rating, precautions, and guidance on next steps.
